> Tromsø harbour fireworks at midnight. **Do not share externally** — the land cost still needs

## Snapshot

- **Duration:** 8 days, 7 nights
- **Fly into / out of:** Tromsø (TOS)
- **Window:** Mon 28 Dec 2026 → Mon 4 Jan 2027
- **Group size:** 16 travellers + 1 OJ trip leader
- **Base split:** Tromsø 5N (incl. New Year's Eve) + Senja island 2N
- **Visa:** Schengen (Norway) — required for Indian passports, ~₹10,150 all-in per applicant
- **Vibe:** Arctic winter, aurora-first. Polar night light, huskies, orcas, and a midnight
  fireworks cruise on the fjord. Low-mileage, high-payoff — not a road trip.

### Why this shape

Tromsø is where the Northern Lights infrastructure, the NYE party and the airport are. It is
also brutally expensive (see costing). Senja is 2.5–3h away by road, roughly **a third of the
nightly rate**, and is the most photogenic coastline in Arctic Norway. Putting NYE in Tromsø
and the quiet days on Senja buys both the party and the margin.

## Day-by-day

**Day 1 — Mon 28 Dec · Land in the Arctic**
Arrive Tromsø late afternoon/evening (polar night — it is dark on landing, and that is the
point). Transfer in, check in, welcome dinner in the harbour quarter. Kit check and a
briefing on layering, because everything after this happens outdoors at −10°C.

**Day 2 — Tue 29 Dec · The city, then the first chase**
Daylight is a four-hour blue window here, so we use it: Fjellheisen cable car up Storsteinen
for the view back over the island and the Arctic Cathedral. Afternoon free in the centre.
After dark, the first **Northern Lights chase** — a small-group minibus that drives wherever
the cloud isn't, sometimes as far as the Finnish border. Thermal suits, a warm meal, 4–6 hours.

**Day 3 — Wed 30 Dec · Whales in the fjords**
Full day north to the Skjervøy fjords for the **silent whale safari** — a hybrid-electric ship
that cuts its engines near the pods, so you hear the blows. Orcas and humpbacks follow the
herring in here through early January. Back to Tromsø by evening.

**Day 4 — Thu 31 Dec · Huskies by day, fireworks at midnight**
Morning: **self-drive dog sledding** in the Villmarkssenter valley — you take the runners
yourself, two to a sled, ~45 minutes across the snow, then a puppy visit and *bidos*
(Sami reindeer stew) by an open fire. Back to town to change.

Night: **New Year's Eve.** Stortorget square fills with live music, ice sculptures and pop-up
bars, and there is a torchlight parade. We board the **NYE fireworks cruise at 23:00** and take
midnight from the water, with the fireworks going up over the Arctic Cathedral and the whole
city skyline reflected in the fjord. Back on the pier around 01:00 for whoever is still going.

**Day 5 — Fri 1 Jan · Out to Senja**
A deliberately slow start. Midday drive south-west to Senja (~3h), Norway's second-largest
island and the one nobody has heard of. Check into the fjord-side base. No city glow out here —
if the sky is clear, the aurora shows up over the water without anyone chasing it.

**Day 6 — Sat 2 Jan · The Senja coast**
The island's greatest hits along the National Scenic Route: **Tungeneset** boardwalk out to
the Devil's Teeth ridge, **Bergsbotn** viewing platform over the fjord, and **Ersfjord** beach
— white sand between black granite walls in the middle of winter. Aurora watch from the base
after dark.

**Day 7 — Sun 3 Jan · Reindeer, and back to town**
Drive back toward Tromsø, stopping at a **Sami camp** in the Lavangsdalen valley: feed the
herd, take a reindeer sled across the snow, then a hot meal in the *lavvu* while the herders
tell you how the family has followed these animals for generations. Last night in Tromsø.

**Day 8 — Mon 4 Jan · Home**
Transfer to TOS and fly out.

## What's included

- 7 nights twin-sharing — 5N Tromsø 4★ city hotel, 2N Senja fjord-side hotel
- Daily breakfast
- OJ trip leader throughout
- Fjellheisen cable car, return
- Northern Lights chase, small-group, with thermal suits and a warm meal
- Silent whale safari to the Skjervøy fjords
- Self-drive dog sledding with bidos lunch, thermal suit and boots
- New Year's Eve fireworks cruise from the harbour
- Sami camp visit — reindeer sledding, feeding and a lavvu meal
- Senja coach leg and all listed viewpoint stops
- Airport transfers on arrival and departure
- Welcome dinner and New Year's Eve dinner

## Not included

- International flights to and from Tromsø (guidance: ₹1.13–1.50L return ex-Delhi, see costing)
- Schengen visa — ~₹10,150 per adult, applied through VFS before flying
- Travel insurance (mandatory for Schengen, €30,000 medical minimum)
- Lunches and dinners other than the three listed above, and all drinks
- Optional add-ons: snowmobile safari at Camp Tamok, Tromsø Ice Domes, aurora dinner cruise
- Tips, personal expenses, GST and gateway charges
