# Norway NYE 2026→27 — Vendor & Activity Cost Research

Research date: **26 Aug 2026**. Priced for **28 Dec 2026 → 4 Jan 2027**, 16 travellers
+ 1 OJ trip leader, twin sharing.

Flights via Kiwi.com. Hotels and activities via **Booking.com live availability** — every
activity line below carries its Booking product ID and review count. Where Booking holds no
inventory (dog sledding, snowmobile, the NYE fireworks cruise, coach charter) the line is
priced operator-direct and marked as such.

> **Correction to an earlier read.** A first pass comparing two *date ranges* appeared to show
> a ~20% New Year surcharge across the board. That was an artifact: a range search returns the
> cheapest date within the range, so a wide range quotes a cheap early-January date and a tight
> New Year range does not. The single-date test above is the reliable one. Price activities on
> the actual date, never on a range.

## 6. Read on this

**Norway over New Year is a genuinely premium product and the sheet should not be forced down
to look like a ₹2L trip.** Three things drive it: Tromsø hotel rates (~NOK 4,000/room/night
minimum, and only 6–30% of that is NYE premium), activity prices that run NOK 1,900–3,400 each
because every one of them includes transfer, thermal kit and a meal, and an NOK that has
appreciated ~23% against the rupee since 2025.

Pricing the activities through Booking.com rather than operator sites changed two lines and
roughly cancelled out: the aurora chase came down (NOK 2,300 → 1,948 for a better-reviewed
minivan product), and the whale safari went **up** (NOK 1,990 → 2,244, since the operator's
published figure is a low-season rate and Booking quotes the actual 31 Dec price). Net effect
on land cost: −₹1,000. The value was not the saving, it was finding that the whale line was
understated.

The levers that actually move the number, in order:

1. **Shift nights to Senja** — ₹13,600/traveller per night moved. Already doing 2N; a third
   is possible if the coach quote lands well.
2. **Trade rates** — the entire sheet is priced at published retail. 10–15% on hotels and
   activities is ~₹30,000 a head and costs nothing but a phone call.
3. **Triple-share rooms** — cuts accommodation ~30% where inventory allows.
4. **Drop the whale safari** — now ₹24,125 a head, and the one activity where the wildlife
   is not guaranteed.
5. **Aurora product choice** — NOK 912 to 2,245 across well-reviewed options, all on the same
   night. Worth ₹14,000 a head between the ends of that range.

What I would not cut: the dog sledding or the NYE fireworks cruise. They are the two things
this trip gets sold on. Worth adding for NOK 302: the **midnight concert in Tromsø Cathedral**
(`PRfoVBatxEFe`, 4.66★) — cheap, and a genuinely distinctive thing to do on New Year's night.
